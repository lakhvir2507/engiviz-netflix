TV Shows vs Movies: A Decade of Change

How has the proportion of TV Shows vs Movies added to Netflix changed over the last decade?

💡

Key Finding

Netflix has dramatically shifted its content strategy over the past decade. In 2011, nearly 100% of new content was Movies. By 2021, TV Shows grew to represent about 35% of new additions — reflecting Netflix's strategic pivot toward original series like Stranger Things, The Crown, and Squid Game that drive sustained subscriber engagement.

Global Content Landscape

Which countries are the largest producers of Netflix content?

🌏

Key Finding

The United States dominates Netflix's library with 2,818 titles — more than the next 5 countries combined. India (972) is the second-largest producer, followed by the United Kingdom (419). Netflix's content strategy is increasingly global, with significant investment in content from Japan, South Korea, Spain, and emerging markets like Nigeria and Turkey.

Genre DNA of Netflix

What are the most common genre combinations?

🎭

Key Finding

The most prevalent genre combination is "International Movies, Dramas" with 362 titles. The word "International" appears in 6 of the top 10 genre tags, highlighting Netflix's global content DNA. Standalone categories like Documentaries (299) and Stand-Up Comedy (273) also have strong presence. Netflix uses 514 unique genre combinations across ~42 base genres to fuel its recommendation engine.

What the Data Tells Us

TV-MA (Mature Audiences) is the most common rating with 3,207 titles, indicating Netflix skews toward adult content. July sees the highest content additions (723 titles), likely timed for summer viewing. The data reveals Netflix as a platform that has evolved from a US-centric movie streamer into a global entertainment powerhouse.

Project Summary

This project explores the Netflix Movies and TV Shows dataset — 8,807 titles spanning content from 1925 to 2021, across 100+ countries and 514 genre combinations. Developed by Team CodeStorm, we uncovered Netflix's strategic shift from movies to series, identified global content production patterns, and mapped the genre landscape that powers Netflix's recommendation engine.

Built with Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly) for data analysis and HTML/CSS/JavaScript with Plotly.js for the interactive web dashboard designed in Netflix Red & White visual style.
